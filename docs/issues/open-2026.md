### Free Ticket modal stranded a completed-battle launch and exposed background controls

**Stable ID:** `ISSUE-2026-041` · **Lifecycle:** `repair_awaiting_confirmation` · **Routing class:** `global_live_preflight_hazard`

- **Observed:** 2026-08-11/12 after an ordinary completed battle returned to
  Home under the managed `NEXT_BATTLE` policy.
- **Symptom:** The runtime verified and dispatched the Home Battle control at
  19:08:39 PDT, then a centered `Free Ticket` / `CLAIM` modal blocked the
  transition. The launch continuation was already consumed even though no
  fresh `RUNNING` boundary had been observed. Home and
  `HOME_AD_GEMS_AVAILABLE` continued to match behind the dimmed modal; the
  Home ad-gem handler exhausted its three local taps, returned, and was
  scheduled again on the next outer heartbeat. By the later investigation it
  had produced 1,002 workflow attempts and 3,004 taps without clearing the
  blocker or advancing the battle.
- **Evidence:** The action log records the completed-battle route, verified
  Battle dispatch, absence of a succeeding active-battle adoption, and the
  repeated Home ad-gem transactions. The retained complete regression frame
  is `test/fixtures/free_ticket_home_20260812.png`; it independently matches
  the Free Ticket title and Claim control while also proving the background
  Home ad-gem visual fact. One later direct capture was incomplete/mostly
  black and was rejected under `ISSUE-2026-022`; an immediate complete retry,
  not that rejected frame, supplied the retained evidence.
- **Cause:** `HOME_SCREEN` remained the selected ordinary primary because the
  modal had no state definition. Terminal-bound continuation authority was
  deleted when the input dispatcher accepted the Battle tap rather than when
  battle lifecycle adoption proved `RUNNING`. The Home ad-gem retry budget was
  local to one handler call, so the App scheduler silently replenished it on
  every equivalent frame. UNKNOWN recovery could not participate because the
  underlying Home match prevented `UNKNOWN`.
- **Safety response:** Diagnosis was passive. Once the operator began a
  Tournament, all live process, control, deployment, and device mutation was
  explicitly withheld; implementation and tests use an isolated feature
  worktree and retained fixtures only.
- **Repair candidate:** Detection now has a nonterminal `blocking_primary`
  class with priority over ordinary screens, and Free Ticket has distinct
  indicator and action templates. App intercepts that state before mission,
  collector, strategy, Home, or UNKNOWN scheduling; only an exact dispatched
  Start or terminal continuation may run the typed, fresh-evidence Claim
  recipe, with Pause/Stop still absolute. Terminal continuation remains a
  process-local postcondition ledger through fresh `RUNNING`; a cleared modal
  can authorize one retry only after two distinct stable exact-Home
  `NEW_BATTLE` observations.
  Explicit Start similarly moves `action_dispatched -> ready` only after the
  known blocker clears. Transient pre-input capture loss defers without
  consuming that owner, while accepted input without an authoritative outcome
  becomes non-replayable. A generic process-local circuit breaker prevents an
  exhausted Home ad-gem transaction from restarting until its target, runtime,
  target generation, or scope changes; OCR control changes are deliberately
  non-material. An uncertain accepted input remains non-replayable even across
  source absence, and one incomplete frame cannot silently rearm it.
- **Regression coverage:** Template workflow validation uses the retained
  source plus ordinary Home, Tournament, and Game Over negatives. Tests cover
  1080 and normalized 720 classification, primary precedence/collision,
  bounded Claim behavior, zero-input authority loss, exact-owner interception,
  retained terminal launch and single retry, explicit Start timeout suspension
  and retry, typed dispatch uncertainty, stable post-input absence, stale-owner
  revocation, and 100 equivalent circuit-broken heartbeats.
- **Status:** Commit `af3d1b0` passed the isolated and exact-`develop`
  checkpoints with 2,391 tests, then reached production on 2026-08-12 behind
  rollback tag `production-before-20260812T192004Z-5c94827`. The managed
  Stop/Start/Attach handoff replaced PID `884966` with `1565008`, rebound the
  same active-battle scope, proved an ordinary Tier 19 save, adopted
  `farm_t19_ad_assist` without degradation, and returned to steady `RUNNING`
  at wave 5762. This validates deployment and process continuity, not the
  incident-specific modal path: confirmation still requires one naturally
  occurring completed-battle Home launch, without manufacturing a battle or
  modal. Actionable confirmation remains in the
  [state/recovery backlog](../backlog/state-and-detection.md#state-coverage-and-recovery).

### T19 Farm retained near-normal game-clock speed while entity throughput collapsed

**Stable ID:** `ISSUE-2026-002` · **Lifecycle:** `confirmed_unresolved` · **Routing class:** `domain_specific`

- **Observed:** 2026-07-29 during a main-PC Tier 19 Farm Retry sequence on
  `localhost:5555`. Nine battles ended at waves 1207–1725 around two longer
  battles ending at waves 4142 and 4732 with only `606.81q` and `676.29q`
  Coins/hour. The operator confirmed that Death Stranding was not active
  during the two longer runs and that Damage Slider remained `100%` throughout
  the sequence.
- **Symptom:** The two longer runs retained effective game speeds of `4.9526`
  and `4.9839`, comparable to `4.9730` and `4.9608` in the surrounding healthy
  main-PC controls, but reported only `192.6` and `196.0` enemies per
  non-skipped wave. The controls reported `270.5` and `275.4`. All nine short
  runs were killed by Scatter after their first survival-ability chain; the
  two longer outliers recorded 18 and 50 Death Defies.
- **Evidence:** The anomalous records are
  `logs/battles/Battle20260729T084914-0700.json` and
  `logs/battles/Battle20260729T124104-0700.json`. Same-tier, same-profile
  controls are `logs/battles/Battle20260728T201308-0700.json` and
  `logs/battles/Battle20260730T083128-0700.json`. Dividing the reported
  `Coins / Kill` by total enemies gives approximately `0.549T` and `0.599T`
  for the anomalies versus `0.588T` and `0.611T` for the controls; Critical
  Coin per enemy is likewise comparable. Golden Tower and Black Hole remained
  active for nearly every reported enemy. The anomalies instead reduced
  largest Golden Combo to 603 and 587 versus 751 and 763, and total coins per
  enemy to `5.34T` and `5.82T` versus `11.11T` and `12.57T`. This supports
  sparse or missed entity processing plus lost combo density rather than a
  missing global coin multiplier or disabled economic Ultimate Weapon.
- **Configuration evidence:** Every retained battle identifies strict
  `farm_t19` / Farm / Tier 19 rather than Dissonance. Session preflight
  verified the selected Farm Cards, Workshop, and Bots presets; all expected
  Modules, Guardians, Ultimate Weapons, and Target Priority; and Auto Pick
  Perks. Final perk records do not show a uniform missing Damage or enemy-health
  tradeoff, and the short records reached the reported maximum Free Attack and
  Defense Upgrade counts. The preflight did not inspect every card inside the
  selected Farm preset, and Free Upgrade locks were deferred because the Retry
  boundary lacked authoritative no-battle Home evidence.
- **Analysis recorded with the 2026-07-29–31 investigation:** A host/emulator
  execution collapse was the leading
  explanation. The game clock and wave schedule appear able to advance near
  normal speed while frame- or update-dependent spawning, attacks, effects, or
  crowd control receive fewer execution opportunities. That model explains
  both lower entity/combo throughput and a Scatter breach instead of uniform
  slowdown, but the game's internal scheduling has not been observed and this
  remains an inference. A stale or altered Farm card deck, especially Enemy
  Balance, is not fully excluded; however, a missing Enemy Balance card alone
  would reduce enemies while normally easing survival, and the retained
  card-derived economic and combat counts argue against a wholesale loadout
  change. The deferred Free Upgrade locks are a weaker unresolved confounder.
- **2026-08-10 operator recurrence and recovery:** The operator reported that
  several recent runs had fallen below prior Coins/hour without known host
  contention and that BlueStacks appeared progressively slower after remaining
  open. Manually stopping and restarting BlueStacks restored performance. This
  is new evidence for an emulator-aging mechanism, but it is not a controlled
  cause attribution: the report has no exact before/after battle cohort and
  contemporaneous retained host window sufficient to exclude every other
  change.
- **Implemented mitigation:** The original candidate in commit `040ca69` was
  forward-ported onto the current control/runtime contracts as the default-off
  revision-39 `bluestacks_maintenance_v1` workflow. It requires two depressed
  same-configuration Farm records at near-normal effective speed, sustained
  same-host/port BlueStacks handle growth across the current Windows sampler
  session, no host CPU/memory saturation, a fresh exact
  runtime/generation/state-request/battle-scope hold, and exact immutable
  Windows executable/instance/port plus listener PID/start-time
  acknowledgement. Linux then relaunches The Tower, handles the separate
  blocking Welcome Back modal, suppresses its non-earning wave rollback, and
  starts a configured new battle after bounded, positively failed Resume.
  Accepted inputs have non-replayable postcondition receipts, and a safely
  restored source releases local input authority even if its terminal reporting
  receipt needs retry. Recovery records are excluded from later trigger
  calibration. The current contract is in the
  [control-surface](../architecture/control_surface.md#automatic-bluestacks-degradation-recovery)
  and [runtime](../architecture/runtime.md#emulator-maintenance-and-restart-replay)
  architecture.
- **Safety response:** The investigation used retained battle records and
  action logs only; it did not alter a live battle or emulator. Commit
  `33b4687` added the passive
  [Windows host-performance telemetry](../architecture/control_surface.md#windows-host-performance-telemetry)
  needed for future correlation without asserting a cause for this historical
  sequence.
- **Durable host evidence:** The exact cited comparison windows and selected
  aggregate fields are preserved in the
  [tracked host-performance extract](evidence/README.md#2026-07-3031-host-performance-windows)
  so rolling database retention cannot remove this evidence.
- **Status:** Confirmed anomaly; exact source unresolved. The automatic
  mitigation remains disabled until its configured BlueStacks executable,
  instance shortcut, and Windows listener are verified and one authorized
  end-to-end Windows recovery is observed. On recurrence,
  correlate the run's UTC/host/ADB intervals with host and BlueStacks CPU,
  memory, power/clock, process, and sampler-cost aggregates. Capture exact card
  membership and Free Upgrade locks at an authoritative new-battle boundary.
  If host counters do not distinguish a healthy run from the anomaly, collect
  one bounded issue-specific frame-timing trace before changing game strategy
  or game-speed policy; do not add a permanent control-surface provider.

  runtime entered the next Home setup and opened Cards. `STOPPED` was persisted
  and acknowledged at 15:19:18, but the process remained alive with its ADB
  lock held because the setup action guard waited indefinitely for input
  authority to become true.
- **Evidence:** `logs/actions.log` records
  `Pause/stop blocked Home setup input; waiting without cleanup actions` after
  the stop acknowledgement. `logs/automation-localhost_5555.lock` continued to
  name PID `2689081` until the foreground runtime received `KeyboardInterrupt`
  at 15:20:24. Static inspection identifies the unbounded
  `while not action_guard_fn()` loop in
  `core/gc_no_battle_setup.py::require_action`; it does not distinguish Pause
  from Stop.

  foreground process was interrupted, releasing its lock; a fresh read then
  identified no-battle `CARDS`, and one separately logged, template-verified
  Home navigation restored `HOME_SCREEN / NEW_BATTLE`.
- **Status:** Deployed production commit `030ad4a` includes feature commit
  `733033b`, which removes the synchronous wait: Pause,
  Stop, and Take Manual Control yield at the first denied input with no cleanup
  action. Exact-owner, runtime, target-generation, and activity-scope binding
  allows at most one later Enabled Home recovery and discards it after Stop or
  ownership change. Server regressions cover Paused and Stopped zero-input
  yield, Take Manual owner change, same-owner recovery, and failed recovery.
  The service/package smoke intentionally left automation STOPPED and sent no
  device input. Keep this dossier open until the deployed runtime confirms
  clean Stop interruption and lock release at a natural Home setup boundary;
  the pending validation remains in
  [`backlog/runtime-and-validation.md`](../backlog/runtime-and-validation.md#runtime-control).

### Long action-log retention made current controls appear pending

**Stable ID:** `ISSUE-2026-038` · **Lifecycle:** `repair_awaiting_confirmation` · **Routing class:** `repair_awaiting_confirmation`

- **Observed:** 2026-08-10 around 13:03 PDT in production status for automation
  PID `1676747`, which was healthy and `RUNNING` in an active battle.
- **Symptom:** The fresh runtime-owned authority snapshot showed ordinary
  Strategy and lifecycle authority, no global Pause, and no Strategy Gate.
  `control_model.strategy_scope` correctly reported startup and active Strategy
  `farm_t19_ad_assist` with no pending next-boundary Strategy. Nevertheless all
  top-level acknowledgements were `null`, so
  `control_model.action_authority.effective` was `pending`.
- **Evidence:** `logs/actions.log` was 5,341,232 bytes. The adapter read only its
  last 262,144 bytes, whose first retained entry was around 07:17 PDT; the
  current state and Strategy acknowledgement messages were around 04:16 PDT.
  The same design would lose them immediately on rotation. This was a status-
  projection defect, not evidence that the live runtime had stopped applying
  directives.
- **Cause:** `ControlSurfaceService.status()` reconstructed current state,
  terminal-policy, speed, ADB, and Strategy acknowledgements from a bounded
  historical log tail. Value/timestamp reconstruction could not provide
  durable exact request identity and was independent of the already-available
  atomic runtime owner channel. WPF then rebuilt current/pending Strategy from
  those compatibility acknowledgements even though it already parsed the
  authoritative Better Control Strategy Scope.
- **Safety response:** Initial investigation and implementation were
  repository-only. Before the later authorized production rollout, no live
  process, device, ADB command, control request, restart, battle action, or
  display-refresh mutation was used. The observed active battle and authority
  semantics were not changed.
- **Repair:** Server revision 37 adds
  `runtime_control_acknowledgements_v1`. The runtime now publishes exact applied
  receipts for state, terminal policy, speed, ADB target, and Strategy inside
  the existing atomically replaced authority snapshot. The envelope must match
  the active held lock's runtime ID, PID, target, and target generation before
  any receipt is exposed; each receipt must also match the current directive's
  value and request ID. Legacy or implicit state/mode/speed fields receive
  exact IDs during runtime startup without an operator request. The action log
  retains its audit messages but grants no current acknowledgement authority.
  Attached-restart readiness and paused ADB handoff consume the same durable
  exact state receipt.
- **Strategy presentation:** The runtime authors startup, active-battle,
